The idea

A villa community inspired by the Hygge way of joyful, sustainable and splendid living. A stone’s throw from the Art of Living, wrapped in forest, built for slow mornings and long evenings.

undo.veer.embrace.
Hygge /ˈhuː.ɡə/ — the Danish art of cosiness, contentment and togetherness.
Five ways of living

Earthy, active, level,
sustainable, together.

Every decision at Saffron Skies, from the brick on the façade to the fruit trees on the path, comes back to one of five ideas.

01

Earthy

Grow your own vegetables. Kota stone underfoot, brick façades turned east and north, courtyards and skylights that let the day in.

02

Active

A jogging track along the boundary, outdoor gym, basketball court, podium yoga lawn and a three-storey clubhouse with pool.

03

Level

Four levels, four moods: social, culinary, relaxation, and a private master suite with its own 400 sq.ft. terrace.

04

Sustainable

EV charging for every home, a 1 lakh litre rainwater tank, groundwater recharge, solar common lighting and on-site greywater treatment.

05

Community

Only 81 families. Verandahs, reading nooks, bistros, tree courts, forest walkways and a temple — a neighbourhood, not a tower.

Open to the garden

Double-height living that steps straight out onto your private garden. Where guests arrive and evenings begin.

Kitchen, dining and the parents’ room on one floor, so the people who cook and the people who matter are never far apart.
